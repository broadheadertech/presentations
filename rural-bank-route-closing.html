How the window closed

Five years of tightening

The doors did not slam. They were narrowed, one issuance at a time.

Dec 2021BSP freezes new EMI licences for non-banks. The acquisition route becomes the only fast way in.
Dec 2023Freeze extended a further year, with narrow exceptions via the regulatory sandbox.
Dec 2024EMI-NBFI moratorium lifted — but applications now need evidence-based market study and tighter fit-and-proper review.
2025Digital bank moratorium lifted with a hard cap of ten licences. The application window then closes again.
Jan–Feb 2026Draft circular on prudential requirements for digital-centric thrift, rural and cooperative banks goes out for comment.
NowIndustry has pushed back on the thresholds. A one-year transition starts the day the final circular takes effect.
Sources: BSP Memoranda M-2021-064, M-2023-035, M-2024-046; BSP Circular 1205 (2024); draft circular on prudential requirements for digital-centric TBs / RBs / Coop Banks, exposure period closed 25 February 2026; RBAP position paper, March 2026.

What is actually happening

Acquisitions are still legal and still happening. What changed is the economics: once a rural bank behaves like a national digital bank, the BSP can require it to be one — with digital-bank capital, governance and supervisory standards attached.

Use the precise version in front of regulators and investors. The loose version is easy to disprove, and it costs you the room.

Cap

30% out-of-area accounts

Customers registered outside the bank’s physical areas of operation may not exceed 30% of total customer accounts.

Trigger

Reclassification

Breach the cap and the BSP can require conversion to a digital bank licence.

Price

₱1 billion capital

Minimum capital on conversion, to be met within one year, alongside a supervisory rating of 3 or better.
